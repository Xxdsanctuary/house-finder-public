About

House Finder is a roommate matching web app built for students. Whether you have a spare room to offer or are looking for a place to stay, you can post a listing, filter by region and budget, and send a request to connect with other users.

Once matched, you can message within your request thread, shortlist listings you like, and leave comments on any listing.
